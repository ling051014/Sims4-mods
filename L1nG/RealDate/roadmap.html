版本規劃

Release Roadmap

Each release brings the passage of time closer to the lives of your Sims.

From dates and real ages to the Calendar, life cycles, and time synchronization, followed by the Life Chronicle, relationship history, Event Management, and anniversaries.

02 · CURRENT FOCUS · v0.6

讓個人的時間，走進社會的節奏。

Connecting personal time with the world around it.

從真實日期與年齡，延伸到學年、入學、放假與畢業；讓人生時間線也能理解社會時間。

From real dates and ages to school years, enrollment, breaks, and graduation—bringing social time into the life timeline.

01

真正的公曆

Gregorian calendar

規劃 365 / 366 天、閏年與 2 月 29 日生日，並保留既有歷史日期。

Planning 365/366-day years, leap years, February 29 birthdays, and preservation of saved historical dates.

02

年齡與時間一致性

Age & time consistency

區分實際年齡、生命階段與學籍，讓調整日期後的狀態能正確重新計算。

Separating real ages, life stages, and student records, with consistent recalculation after date changes.

03

學年與學校生活

Academic years & school life

規劃 6 歲入學、12 年學制、國中、南北半球學年與真正的寒暑假。

Planning school entry at age six, twelve school years, middle school, hemisphere-based academic calendars, and school breaks.

03 · LONG-TERM VISION

讓一段人生，留在同一條時間線上。

Let a whole life live on one timeline.

RealDate 的長期方向，是逐步連結模擬市民的日期、年齡、生命經歷與重要記憶。各階段會隨技術驗證及實際開發調整。

Our long-term direction is to connect dates, age, experiences and memories. Each stage may change with technical feasibility.
