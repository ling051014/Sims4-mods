02 · CURRENT FOCUS

讓重要的一天，不只留下日期。

Let important days leave more than dates.

Build on the existing time system so important events have a history.

Life events

03 · LONG-TERM VISION

讓一段人生，留在同一條時間線上。

Let a whole life live on one timeline.

RealDate 的長期方向，是逐步連結模擬市民的日期、年齡、生命經歷與重要記憶。各階段會隨技術驗證及實際開發調整。

Our long-term direction is to connect dates, age, experiences and memories. Each stage may change with technical feasibility.
