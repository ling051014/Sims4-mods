THE SIMS 4 · TIME & LIFE SYSTEM

REALDATE

讓《模擬市民 4》裡的時間，不只是一天一天經過。

Make time in The Sims 4 feel like a life that actually moves forward.

RealDate 從真實年月日出發，把出生日期、實際年齡、生日、月曆、 季節、生理與懷孕時間軸、年號與生活玩法逐步連接到同一條時間線。

RealDate starts with real calendar dates and connects birth dates, real ages, birthdays, the Calendar, seasons, reproductive and pregnancy timelines, Eras, and life gameplay to one shared timeline.
