提取內容WHAT YOU CAN EXPORT

把遊戲裡的家族資料，帶進自己的族譜。

Bring your in-game family history into your own tree.

選擇想匯出的存檔，提取器就會整理其中可取得的資料。實際內容會依人物、存檔和已安裝的模組而有所不同。

The exporter collects available information from your selected save. Results vary by Sim, save, and installed mods.

使用方式HOW TO USE

從遊戲到族譜，只要四個步驟。

Four steps from your game to your family tree.

公開下載尚未開放。以下是目前規劃的使用流程；下載開放後，請以當時提供的安裝檔與說明為準。

Public downloads are not available yet. This is the planned workflow; follow the installation instructions supplied with the released files.

01

安裝族譜提取器

Install Genealogy Exporter

將提供的 .ts4script 與 .pyd 放進 Mods 內同一個資料夾。.pyd 請保持為獨立檔案，不要壓縮或放入 .ts4script。遊戲中也要啟用「自訂內容與模組」及「允許腳本模組」。

Place the supplied .ts4script and .pyd in the same Mods subfolder. Keep the .pyd as a separate, uncompressed file. Enable Custom Content & Mods and Script Mods in the game.

02

進入要匯出的遊戲存檔

Load your chosen save

進入生活模式，等待存檔載入完成。如需確認提取器是否成功啟動，可以輸入右側指令。

Enter Live Mode and wait for the save to load. You can use the command on the right to check whether the exporter is ready.

03

執行族譜匯出

Export your family tree

開啟遊戲指令輸入框，輸入右側指令。等待匯出完成，期間請不要旅行或切換存檔。完成後可依顯示的路徑找到 ZIP。

Open the in-game command console and enter the command on the right. Wait until export finishes without travelling or changing saves, then locate the ZIP at the reported path.

04

匯入網頁族譜工具

Import into Genealogy

開啟族譜工具，選擇「更多 → 匯入遊戲族譜」，再選取剛剛產生的 ZIP。網站會自動處理可取得的模擬市民與寵物頭像。

Open Genealogy, choose More → Import Game Genealogy, and select your ZIP. The website automatically handles available Sim and pet portraits.

開啟族譜工具 → Open Genealogy →
預設輸出位置 Default export folder Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\L1nG_Genealogy_Exports

每次匯出會建立一份帶有日期時間的 L1nG_Genealogy_*.zip，請選取這個 ZIP 匯入網站。

Each export creates a dated L1nG_Genealogy_*.zip. Select that ZIP when importing into the website.

匯出檔案YOUR EXPORT FILE

一份 ZIP，保存這次提取的族譜資料。

One ZIP for the family-tree data you exported.

ZIP 內包含人物、家庭與族譜資料，以及當次取得的頭像。這是提供給網頁族譜工具的資料檔，不是遊戲存檔備份。檔案會保存在你的電腦中，只有你自行選取時才會匯入網站；缺少頭像的剪影不代表人物的真實外貌。

The ZIP contains Sims, households, family-tree information, and any available portraits. It is not a backup of your game save. The file stays on your computer until you choose to import it. Placeholder silhouettes are not real portraits.

哪些資料能帶進族譜？哪些可能缺少？

What can be imported, and what might be missing?

可取得的資料取決於遊戲存檔與人物狀態。即使沒有完整頭像或個人資料，仍可保留可讀取的族譜關係。

Availability depends on your save and each Sim's state. Family relationships can still be preserved when portraits or full details are missing.

主要支援內容 Included Information
  • 人物、家庭與親子族譜關係
  • Sims, households, and family relationships
  • 可取得的特徵、職業與其他人物資料
  • Available traits, careers, and other Sim details
  • 貓狗及可取得的遊戲原生頭像
  • Cats, dogs, and available native portraits
  • 原本存在於遊戲族譜的歷代家族成員
  • Relatives retained in the game's family tree
  • ZIP 匯出及網頁族譜工具匯入
  • ZIP export and website import
部分內容可能缺少 Some Details May Be Missing
  • 不是每位模擬市民或寵物都一定有頭像
  • Not every Sim or pet has an available portrait
  • 部分特殊種族、混合狀態或已故人物的頭像可能無法取得
  • Some occult, mixed-state, or deceased Sims may have no portrait
  • 只留在族譜中的人物可能沒有完整個人資料
  • Family-tree-only relatives may lack full details
  • 目前不提供家庭合照提取
  • Household group portraits are not supported
  • 缺少頭像時顯示預設剪影
  • Missing portraits display a placeholder silhouette
