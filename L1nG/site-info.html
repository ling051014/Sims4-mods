隱私權與 Cookie隐私权与 CookiePrivacy & Cookies

適用範圍

本隱私權政策適用於 L1nG 的公開網站及相關專案頁面，包括首頁、RealDate、族譜工具、族譜提取器與漢化專區。

本站僅基於網站運作、流量分析、安全防護及問題回饋等目的處理相關資料。以下說明本站如何蒐集、使用、儲存及保護訪客與玩家的資料。

網站流量分析

本站使用 Google Analytics 4（GA4）統計網站瀏覽量、流量來源、國家或地區及裝置資訊，以了解網站使用情況並改善使用體驗。

GA4 加強型評估可能記錄頁面捲動、外部連結點擊及檔案下載。我們另外統計 RealDate、漢化專區及其他支援的下載連結點擊次數，以及問題回饋視窗開啟、成功送出等操作事件。

這些統計僅用於了解網站使用趨勢及改善功能。下載點擊不代表檔案已成功下載，本站也不會主動將問題回饋內容、附件、聯絡資訊或匿名對話憑證傳送至 GA4。

相關分析資料由 Google 依其服務條款及隱私權政策處理。開發及分支預覽環境不會啟用正式 GA4 統計。

Cookie 與偏好設定

本站使用 Cookie 或類似技術維持網站基本功能、保存使用者偏好及進行流量分析。

  • 必要功能：記住 Cookie 偏好設定，並維持網站基本運作
  • 分析統計：使用 Google Analytics 4 了解網站流量及使用情況

本站會記住你的 Cookie 偏好設定，有效期限為一年，並在 L1nG 相關網站間共用。GA4 使用的 Cookie 則依 Google Analytics 設定及瀏覽器限制保存。

本站會依訪客所在地區決定分析統計的啟用方式。依法需要事先同意的地區，在取得同意前不會載入 GA4；其他經評估可採拒絕制的地區，可能預設啟用分析統計。若無法確認所在地區，則會先徵詢同意。

你可以隨時透過 Cookie 偏好設定接受、拒絕或撤回分析統計同意。拒絕或撤回後，本站會停止後續分析統計，並嘗試清除可管理的相關 Cookie。

你也可以透過瀏覽器隱私設定限制或清除 Cookie，或使用 Google Analytics 停用工具（如瀏覽器支援）。

瀏覽器儲存與族譜資料

部分網站功能會將語言、顯示偏好及其他設定保存在目前裝置的瀏覽器中。

族譜工具建立、匯入或編輯的市民資料、家庭關係及圖片，也可能儲存在瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。一般使用族譜編輯功能時，這些內容不會自動上傳至問題回饋服務，也不會作為 GA4 分析資料傳送。

清除瀏覽器網站資料、更換裝置或使用無痕模式，可能導致尚未備份的資料遺失。建議定期使用工具提供的匯出功能保存重要內容。

問題回饋與附件

使用 RealDate 或族譜工具的問題回饋功能時，本站會處理你主動提供的資料，包括問題描述、回饋類型、模組與遊戲版本、上傳附件，以及選填的聯絡資訊。

如果選擇持續追蹤問題，相關的匿名對話與回覆紀錄也會一併保存。

這些資料僅用於調查問題、改善作品、回覆玩家及管理回饋紀錄，並透過 Cloudflare 提供的服務儲存與處理。

本站不要求登入帳號即可提交問題回饋。若選擇匿名對話，系統會提供私人憑證供你日後查看回覆，伺服器不會儲存原始憑證。

為防止垃圾訊息及濫用行為，本站使用 Cloudflare Turnstile 進行安全驗證，並可能依連線來源及請求次數進行頻率限制。

請勿在回饋內容或附件中提交與問題無關的敏感個人資料。

Email 回覆通知

如果你主動提供電子郵件地址並選擇啟用 Email 回覆通知，本站會在相關服務啟用時寄送驗證郵件。完成驗證後，當管理員回覆問題時，系統便可透過電子郵件提醒你。

這項功能使用 Resend 提供郵件寄送服務，相關電子郵件地址及必要的通知內容會交由該服務處理。

Email 通知僅用於問題回饋相關的驗證與回覆提醒，不會用於寄送行銷郵件。你可以透過通知郵件中的連結關閉後續通知。

關閉 Email 通知不代表原有的問題回饋及聯絡資料會立即刪除。如需刪除相關資料，可以透過問題回饋功能提出申請。

未啟用 Email 通知的玩家，仍可使用私人憑證查看匿名對話及管理員回覆。

本站依功能需求使用以下第三方服務：

  • Cloudflare：網站運作、安全防護、人機驗證及問題回饋資料處理
  • Resend：啟用 Email 通知時提供郵件驗證及回覆提醒

相關資料可能由服務提供者依其隱私權政策及服務條款處理，並可能涉及台灣以外地區的資料傳輸或儲存。

資料保存與安全

本站會依資料用途、問題處理需求及相關服務設定保存必要資訊。

Scope of this policy

This privacy policy applies to L1nG's public websites and project pages, including the home page, RealDate, Genealogy Tool, Genealogy Exporter, and Translations.

We process information only for purposes such as operating the websites, analyzing traffic, protecting security, and handling feedback. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information from visitors and players.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure page views, traffic sources, countries or regions, and device information so we can understand how our websites are used and improve the experience.

GA4 enhanced measurement may record scrolling, outbound-link clicks, and file downloads. We also count clicks on supported RealDate, translation, and other download links, as well as actions such as opening feedback forms and successfully submitting feedback.

These statistics are used to understand usage trends and improve features. A download click does not mean a file was downloaded successfully. We do not intentionally send feedback messages, attachments, contact details, or private conversation receipts to GA4.

Google processes analytics data under its terms and privacy policy. Production GA4 tracking is not enabled in development or branch previews.

Cookies and preferences

We use cookies or similar technologies to support basic website functions, remember preferences, and analyze traffic.

  • Essential functions：Remember your cookie preferences and support core site functionality
  • Analytics：Use Google Analytics 4 to understand website traffic and usage

We remember your cookie preferences for one year and share this preference across related L1nG sites. GA4 cookies are retained according to Google Analytics settings and browser restrictions.

Whether analytics is enabled by default depends on the visitor's region. In regions that require prior consent, GA4 will not load before consent is given. In other regions assessed as permitting opt-out, analytics may be enabled by default. If the region cannot be determined, we ask for consent first.

You can accept, reject, or withdraw consent for analytics at any time through Cookie preferences. After you reject or withdraw consent, the site stops subsequent analytics tracking and attempts to remove related cookies under its control.

You can also restrict or clear cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (if supported by your browser).

Browser storage and genealogy data

Some website features save language, display preferences, and other settings in the browser on your device.

Sim information, family relationships, and images created, imported, or edited in Genealogy Tool may also be stored locally in your browser. During ordinary use of the genealogy editor, this content is not automatically uploaded to the feedback service or sent as GA4 analytics data.

Clearing browser site data, changing devices, or using private browsing may cause unbacked-up information to be lost. We recommend exporting backups of important content regularly.

Feedback and attachments

If you use the RealDate or Genealogy Tool feedback feature, we process information you choose to submit, including your message, feedback category, mod and game versions, uploaded attachments, and optional contact details.

If you choose to continue a conversation, related anonymous conversation and reply records are also retained.

This information is used only to investigate issues, improve our projects, reply to players, and manage feedback records, and is stored and processed using Cloudflare services.

You do not need an account to submit feedback. If you choose an anonymous conversation, you receive a private receipt to view subsequent replies. The server does not store the original receipt.

To prevent spam and abuse, we use Cloudflare Turnstile for security checks and may enforce rate limits based on connection sources and request counts.

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information in feedback messages or attachments.

Email reply notifications

If you voluntarily provide an email address and choose to enable email reply notifications, we send a verification email when this service is available. Once verified, the system can notify you by email when an administrator replies to your feedback.

This feature uses Resend for email delivery. Your email address and the necessary notification content are processed by this service.

Email notifications are only used for feedback-related verification and reply alerts, not marketing emails. You can disable future notifications using the link in a notification email.

Disabling email notifications does not immediately delete your existing feedback or contact details. You can request deletion through the feedback feature.

Players who do not enable email notifications can still view anonymous conversations and administrator replies using their private receipt.

Third-party services and external links

We use the following third-party services as needed:

  • Google Analytics 4：Website traffic and usage analytics
  • Cloudflare：Website operations, security, human verification, and feedback data processing
  • Resend：Email verification and reply alerts when email notifications are enabled

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies, and data may be transferred to or stored outside Taiwan.

We also link to mod creators' websites, Dropbox, Patreon, AFDIAN, and other third-party websites. When you leave our websites, those sites' own privacy and cookie policies apply.

GA4 outbound-link measurement may record the destination URLs you click.

Data retention and security

We retain necessary information based on its purpose, feedback-handling needs, and the settings of the services involved.

Cookie preferences are saved for one year. Analytics data is retained according to the actual Google Analytics settings and the service's retention rules.

Feedback, conversation records, attachments, and optional contact details are kept as needed for issue handling and related administration. There is currently no single automatic deletion period for all feedback data.

You can contact L1nG to request deletion. We handle requests according to the nature of the data and applicable requirements. Data held by third-party services may be subject to their own retention and deletion rules.

We use security verification, access controls, and other appropriate measures to reduce risks of unauthorized access, data breaches, or misuse.

Your privacy rights and contacting us

If you have questions about our data practices or want to access, correct, stop the use of, or delete personal information you provided, you can contact L1nG through the RealDate or Genealogy Tool feedback feature.

To protect data, we may need to verify that the information belongs to you and will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy in response to changes in website features, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page. We recommend checking it periodically for the latest information about how data is handled.
